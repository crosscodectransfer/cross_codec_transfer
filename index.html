(a) Each codec maps speech x of duration T to riT frames with Ki codebooks. Tokens are used at their native rate or stacked to match Codec 0’s rate r0. (b) The speech language model is pretrained on text and Codec 0 tokens, with a depth Transformer predicting the codes within each frame. (c) Transfer freezes the middle Transformer layers and trains the outer layers, the target-codec embeddings and the depth Transformer on Codec 1 tokens.
