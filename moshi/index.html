Moshi
what each swapped-codec model answered

Moshiko (Kyutai's speech-to-speech dialogue model) retrained to hear and speak through a different audio codec instead of Mimi: the Qwen3-TTS tokenizer (16 codebooks, 12.5 Hz) or JHCodec (8 codebooks, four 50 Hz frames stacked into one 12.5 Hz step). Each was trained for 5 epochs on 240k synthetic spoken QA dialogues, with all 32 temporal layers trainable (freeze0) or the middle 24 kept at moshiko's weights (freeze24). The header plays the question as the models heard it; each row plays the whole exchange (question and the model's spoken answer, mixed) and shows the model's own text stream from the moment the question starts. The table under each tab gives the scores over the full benchmark.
